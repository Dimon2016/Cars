Facts about Audi R8

The Audi R8 is a mid-engine supercar produced by Audi from 2006 to 2024, sharing much of its engineering with the Lamborghini Huracán in later generations. It became famous for combining exotic-car performance with everyday drivability, featuring Audi’s quattro all-wheel-drive system and naturally aspirated V8 and V10 engines. The top versions produced over 600 horsepower and could accelerate from 0–60 mph in about 3 seconds, with top speeds exceeding 200 mph. The R8 also gained pop-culture recognition after appearing as Tony Stark’s car in the Iron Man movies. Audi offered both coupe and Spyder convertible versions, and the car was praised for its sharp handling, distinctive LED lighting, and gated manual transmission in early models, which later became highly collectible among enthusiasts.
